# Permission and disclosure failures

## An optional permission disables the whole feature

Limit or deny first-use access and complete an unrelated local action. For an empty HealthKit read, show what the query returned. A write authorization result cannot settle whether the person granted read access. Retry offline and confirm pending work remains visible until completion. Check the exact platform, data type, and permission before offering recovery.

## Tracking consent breaks receipt lookup

A receipt endpoint and an SDK tracker share a domain listed in `NSPrivacyTrackingDomains`. On systems that enforce the manifest, denying tracking can break both requests. Move receipt lookup to a dedicated host that performs no tracking. With consent denied, verify the receipt loads and inspect network activity for unintended tracking.

## A deletion claim exceeds what the app controls

Compare the in-app deletion flow with the inventory of retained copies. Follow one account through completion and verify which app-controlled records disappear. Describe the fate of user-controlled exports separately.
